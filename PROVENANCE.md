# His photographs: where each one is shown from

Every photograph in the photography page's gallery is the work of **Alexander Saladrigas** (TMade Alex), from the
*Personal* page of his own photography site. They are his. **All rights reserved.** They are not covered by any licence
this repository carries.

**None of them is copied into this repository.** The gallery shows each one from the picture host of his own site
(`images.squarespace-cdn.com`, the addresses his Personal page uses), at a width asked for with `?format=`: 750 px for
the grid, 1500 px for a tall picture opened large and 2500 px for a wide one. The list is
[`src/data/gallery.ts`](./src/data/gallery.ts). If he takes a picture down from his site, it goes from here too, and
emptying that list takes them all off this one.

- Chosen: his personal work, ahead of client and magazine work. No magazine cover and no brand campaign.

- Shown: each one is credited *Photograph: Alexander Saladrigas* in the picture viewer, with a link back to
  <https://www.alexandersaladrigas.com/doc>, and the gallery carries the same credit underneath. Nothing was changed: no
